Privacy Policy

DROOM IT collects only the information needed to respond to enquiries, prepare website audits and discuss potential projects.

Information we collect

When you submit a contact form or website audit request, DROOM IT may collect your name, company, email address, website URL, project type and message.

How the information is used

The information is used to answer your request, prepare a project recommendation, schedule a consultation or follow up about the service you asked about.

Storage and processors

Form submissions may be processed through the website hosting and database infrastructure used by DROOM IT. Access is limited to project communication and administration.

Email communication

If you contact DROOM IT, you may receive a direct reply related to your enquiry. DROOM IT does not sell contact details or send unrelated marketing emails from these forms.

Cookies and analytics

The website uses only basic technical browser storage for language preference. No non-essential analytics or marketing cookies are currently loaded by this website.

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your personal information by emailing info@droomit.be.
