E-commerce

Product pages that make buying decisions easier.

DROOM IT designs product-focused experiences for shops and B2B product businesses that need customers to compare, understand and enquire with less friction.

Who it is for

Small shops, product companies and B2B suppliers that need a clearer product catalogue or smoother sales path.

What it solves

Confusing product structure, weak product pages, difficult mobile browsing and unclear routes to checkout or enquiry.

Typical deliverables

Product hierarchy, UX/UI, responsive product pages, enquiry or checkout flow, content structure and launch support.

Process

Product structure comes before visual polish.

  1. Map product categories, buyer questions and decision points.
  2. Design product listing and detail pages around comparison and confidence.
  3. Build the enquiry or checkout path with responsive UX.
  4. Launch with SEO basics and clear product navigation.

FAQ

Can you design catalogue-style websites?

Yes. Not every product business needs immediate checkout; product discovery and enquiry flows can be designed around the sales process.

Can product pages include technical documents?

Yes. Technical resources can be structured so buyers can find the information they need.

Can the site be multilingual?

Yes. Product and category pages can be planned for English and Dutch audiences.
