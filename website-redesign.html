Website redesign

When your website no longer reflects your business.

DROOM IT helps companies replace outdated pages with a clearer structure, stronger visual hierarchy and responsive experience that feels easier for customers to use.

Who it is for

Businesses with a website that feels old, confusing, too slow or disconnected from the quality of the company.

What it solves

Weak first impressions, unclear offers, poor mobile layouts, low confidence and contact forms that do not support enquiries well.

Typical deliverables

Website audit, revised sitemap, UX/UI direction, rewritten page structure, responsive rebuild, QA and launch support.

Process

Redesign starts with diagnosis, not decoration.

  1. Review the current website, audience and conversion path.
  2. Identify structural, content and mobile usability issues.
  3. Redesign the priority pages and rebuild the experience.
  4. Test, launch and monitor practical improvements.

FAQ

Can you audit the current site first?

Yes. A website audit can identify the most important problems before a full redesign is proposed.

Do we need to rewrite everything?

Not always. Strong existing content can be kept, reorganized or edited where it helps the visitor.

Can the redesign stay multilingual?

Yes. English and Dutch structure can be planned from the start.
